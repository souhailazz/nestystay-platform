Breeze-block texture — dark sections only
Geometric claustra SVG, 3–5% strokes in production (shown at 8% here), applied with a fade — never full-bleed at uniform opacity. Doctor-bird V silhouettes (1.6px strokes, 35% opacity) mark dark-section skies.
Motion principles (from PUB-01)
· Reveals: translateY 24px + fade, 700ms ease-out, 100ms stagger, IntersectionObserver — hidden states only under html.js (full page without JS)
· Loops: slow (4–8s), CSS-only — float ±8px, glow pulses, sways. Nothing nervous.
· Parallax: translateY only, data-depth 0.1–0.92, ≤60px amplitude, off ≤700px & reduced-motion
· Hovers: arrows slide +4px; photos zoom 1.03; nav underline scaleX yellow
· Only transform & opacity animate. Every animation annotated <!-- anim: … -->
